Built around one case file

Everything a patient does and everything a doctor sees pulls from the same record — no re-typing, no lost paper.

📝

Guided case-taking

Structured intake with voice or text, mapped to clinical codes as the patient answers.

🧠

AI-drafted summaries

Every case gets a confidence-scored draft summary — the doctor always makes the final call.

📄

Prescription scanner

Old paper prescriptions and lab reports are read automatically, in Hindi, English or a mix.

👥

Live OPD queue

Reception and doctors see who's waiting, who's in consultation, and how long the line is — in real time.

📚

Complete medical history

Patients log in and see every past visit, diagnosis and prescription in one timeline.

💬

Live support assistant

Answers common questions about timings and visit prep, so the front desk isn't interrupted — 24×7.
